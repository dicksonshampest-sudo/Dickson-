👤 About Dickson

Learn more about the artist.

Dickson Shampest

Dickson Shampest is a singer-songwriter from Zambia. His music blends soulful melodies with contemporary African rhythms, creating a sound that is both timeless and fresh. With a passion for storytelling, his songs explore love, life, and the human experience.

He has performed at numerous festivals and venues across Africa and continues to captivate audiences with his powerful voice and heartfelt lyrics.

His music is available on all major streaming platforms. Stay tuned for more!
